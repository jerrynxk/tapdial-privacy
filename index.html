一句话承诺：TapDial 是一款完全在本机运行的拨号辅助工具。它没有账号体系、没有广告、默认不联网，也不需要也不上传您的任何个人信息。

✓ 完全本地运行 ✓ 无账号 ✓ 无广告 ✓ 不联网 ✓ 不收集个人信息

1. 我们收集哪些信息

什么都不收集。TapDial 没有注册、登录或用户账户体系，不会要求您提供姓名、手机号、邮箱、身份证号等任何身份信息。我们也没有任何服务器来接收或保存您的数据。

应用运行时读取的通讯录、拨号记录等数据，仅用于在本机屏幕上完成显示、检索与拨号，绝不会上传到任何外部服务。

2. 完全本地运行

  • 联系人、通话记录、快捷拨号、设置偏好等全部保存在您的 iPhone / iPad 本机（App Group 沙盒）中。
  • 卸载 App 即可彻底删除本机保存的全部数据，不会在云端留下任何副本。
  • 应用不使用任何第三方统计、广告、崩溃上报或用户画像 SDK。

3. 无账号 · 无广告 · 不联网

  • 无账号：打开即用，不需要注册或登录。
  • 无广告：应用内没有任何广告位、广告 SDK 或个性化推荐。
  • 不联网：核心功能（拨号、T9 搜索、联系人、快捷拨号、外观设置）均在本机完成，应用不会主动向互联网发起任何数据请求。

唯一例外是下面第 4 条中、由您主动开启的 iCloud 设置同步；即使开启，数据也只经由 Apple 官方通道在您自己的设备间同步，不经过我们，我们无法读取。

4. 可选功能：iCloud 设置同步

设置页提供「把设置同步到 iCloud」开关，默认关闭。只有您手动开启后，应用才会通过 Apple 提供的 iCloud 键值存储（NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore）在您自己的设备之间同步设置快照（如皮肤、字号、布局偏好）。

  • 该功能依赖您自己的 iCloud 账户，由 Apple 提供并受 Apple 隐私政策约束。
  • 同步内容不包含通讯录、通话记录等个人信息。
  • 我们既不运营同步服务器，也无法访问您的 iCloud 数据。
  • 您可以随时在系统设置或应用设置中关闭此功能。

5. 系统权限的使用

  • 通讯录权限：用于在本机显示联系人、生成 T9 检索与快捷拨号；数据不离开设备。
  • 拨号能力：点击号码后跳转到系统拨号界面完成呼叫，应用不代替您自动拨号、不录音、不监听通话。

6. 儿童隐私

TapDial 适合所有年龄段的用户，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。我们不会收集任何人（包括儿童）的任何信息。

7. 条款更新

Our promise in one sentence: TapDial is a dialing utility that runs entirely on your device. It has no account system, no ads, no network activity by default, and never asks for or uploads any of your personal information.

✓ Fully local ✓ No account ✓ No ads ✓ No network ✓ No personal data collected

1. What we collect

Nothing. TapDial has no registration, login or user account system, and never asks for your name, phone number, email, ID number or any other identity information. We also have no server to receive or store your data.

Contacts and call records read by the app are used only for on-device display, search and dialing, and are never uploaded to any external service.

2. Fully local

  • Contacts, call history, speed dial and preferences are all stored on your iPhone / iPad (App Group sandbox).
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data; no copy is left in the cloud.
  • The app uses no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or profiling SDKs.

3. No account, no ads, no network

  • No account: use it right away — no sign-up or login required.
  • No ads: no ad placements, ad SDKs or personalized recommendations.
  • No network: all core features (dialing, T9 search, contacts, speed dial, appearance) run locally; the app never initiates an outbound data request.

The only exception is the optional iCloud sync in section 4 that you enable yourself. Even when enabled, data syncs only through Apple's official channels between your own devices — it never passes through us and we cannot read it.

4. Optional: iCloud sync

Settings offers a "Sync settings to iCloud" switch, off by default. Only after you turn it on does the app sync a settings snapshot (skins, font size, layout preferences) between your own devices via Apple's iCloud key-value store (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore).

  • The feature relies on your own iCloud account, is provided by Apple, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Synced content does not include contacts, call history or other personal information.
  • We operate no sync server and cannot access your iCloud data.
  • You can turn it off at any time in system or app settings.

5. Permissions we use

  • Contacts: used only to display contacts locally, build T9 search and speed dial; data never leaves the device.
  • Dialing: tapping a number hands off to the system dialer to place the call; the app never dials automatically, records audio or listens to calls.

6. Children's privacy

TapDial is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. We do not collect any information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. If it changes, we will update the "Updated" date on this page. Since the app collects no user information, no change is ever applied retroactively to data already on your device.
